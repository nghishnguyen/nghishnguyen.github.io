Case 01 · Experimental research

How Communication Format Shapes Informational and Social Decisions

A controlled experiment tested whether receiving the same message through live video or voice-only communication changed belief revision (i.e., epistemic decision) and wait time on the Delay-of-gratification task (i.e., social decision -- waiting longer means that a child trusts that a speaker would fulfill her stated promise).

Experiment · Behavioral measures · GLMMs · Time-to-event analysis

Case 02 · Strategic research

How Concrete Environment Predicts Children's Various Decisions

An association study examined how residential mobility (i.e., frequency of moving homes within childhood) and group membership shape whom children rely on to (1) Learn new information (2) Revise existing beliefs.

Experimental design · Survey research · Context-sensitive segmentation

Case 03 · Evidence synthesis

Turning a Fragmented Literature Into a Decision-Ready Evidence Map

A published scoping review organized inconsistent definitions and measures of effort in mathematics into a framework for research, measurement, and intervention.

Literature search · Screening · Evidence extraction · Synthesis

About

Behavioral evidence with a practical destination.

My research examines how communication channels, prior beliefs, social environments, and motivational contexts shape people’s decisions. I am especially interested in questions involving trust, information use, engagement, and adaptation.

Across my work, I balance theoretical precision with the needs of real decision-makers. That means identifying the mechanism a study can explain, the experience or intervention it could inform, and the evidence still needed before claiming impact.

I translate complex human behavior into research that teams can use.

Research approach

From question to evidence to action

I lead research across the full lifecycle while selecting methods that fit the decision—not the other way around.

Frame

Translate broad behavioral or experience problems into focused, testable research questions and measurable constructs.

Investigate

Design experiments, surveys, behavioral tasks, and evidence reviews; coordinate recruitment, protocols, and data quality.

Apply

Separate verified findings from hypotheses, then identify product, UX, program, intervention, or communication decisions to test.
